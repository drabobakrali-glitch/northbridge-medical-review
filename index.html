FAQ

Before you contact us.

Do you provide emergency medical advice?

No. This is a document explanation and review service. For urgent symptoms or emergencies, contact your local emergency service or treating clinician.

Can you diagnose me or change my medication?

No. We do not replace your treating doctor, provide emergency care or independently prescribe/change treatment through this service.

Are translations certified?

No. Unless explicitly agreed otherwise, translations are non-certified, patient-friendly medical translations.

How do I get a price?

Send the document and your request through WhatsApp. We confirm the scope and price before starting.
