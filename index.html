Frequently asked questions

Can I send my medical report by WhatsApp?

Not through a personal WhatsApp account. We will provide an appropriate secure submission method after your request is assessed.

Do you provide emergency medical advice?

No. This is not an emergency service. Please contact your local emergency or healthcare service for urgent symptoms.

Will you change my medication?

Not through the translation or explanation service. Any clinical service will have a separately defined scope and will only be offered where the relevant requirements are satisfied.

Do you cover every medical specialty?

No. Our initial specialist focus is Gastroenterology and Hepatology.

Can I pay online?

The production version can use a payment provider such as Stripe after the service scope is confirmed. This demo does not collect payment information.
